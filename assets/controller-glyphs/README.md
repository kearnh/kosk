# Controller glyphs

The SVGs in `kenney/` are unmodified vectors from Kenney's
[Input Prompts 1.5A](https://kenney.nl/assets/input-prompts), licensed under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The upstream notice is preserved in [LICENSE.txt](LICENSE.txt).

Source: [official archive](https://kenney.nl/media/pages/assets/input-prompts/8de120163f-1783763952/kenney_input-prompts_1.5.zip), downloaded 2026-10-03.
Archive SHA-256: `ac2fcf599080b0f3ba2d174c9474db6df1a0e96ff0662580e2da79a122ab78a1`.

Steam Controller artwork comes from `Steam Controller/Vector/`; its square
left/right pad and stick-click prompts come from `Steam Deck/Vector/`.
DualShock 4 artwork comes from `PlayStation Series/Vector/`; its system button
uses the home symbol from `Nintendo Switch/Vector/switch_button_home.svg`.
Rear buttons and Quick Access use Steam Controller artwork for both families.
